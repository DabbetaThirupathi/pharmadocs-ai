# User Access Management for GxP Computerized Systems

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To control user access to GxP computerized systems such as ERP, LIMS and QMS in line with 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11.
## 2. Access Request
Access is requested through an access request form approved by the user's head of department and the system owner.
## 3. Unique Accounts
Each user has a unique user ID. Shared or generic accounts are not permitted.
## 4. Periodic Review
User access is reviewed every 6 months, and accounts of employees who leave are disabled within 24 hours.
## 5. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 8 characters, changed every 90 days, and accounts lock after 3 failed attempts.
