# Monitoring of Differential Pressure in Cleanrooms

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To ensure cleanrooms maintain pressure cascades that protect the product from contamination.
## 2. Limits
A differential pressure of at least 10 Pa must be maintained between adjacent rooms of different cleanroom grades, with air flowing from the higher grade to the lower grade.
## 3. Monitoring
Differential pressure is monitored continuously by the building management system, with alarms when the value falls below 10 Pa for more than 5 minutes.
## 4. Alarm Response
On an alarm, stop open aseptic operations, close doors, inform engineering and QA, and record the event. Raise a deviation if the excursion lasted more than 5 minutes during operation.
