# Change Control

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To ensure changes to facilities, equipment, processes, documents and computerized systems are evaluated and approved before implementation.
## 2. Change Request
The initiator raises a change request describing the current state, the proposed change and the justification.
## 3. Impact Assessment
Cross-functional reviewers assess the impact on product quality, validation status, regulatory filings and documentation.
## 4. Classification
Major changes require QA Head approval and may need regulatory notification. Minor changes are approved by the department head and QA.
## 5. Closure
The change is closed after all actions are completed and effectiveness is verified.
