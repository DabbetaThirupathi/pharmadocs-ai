# Handling of Deviations

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To define how unplanned deviations from approved procedures, specifications or GMP requirements are reported, classified, investigated and closed.
## 2. Reporting
Any employee who observes a deviation must inform the shift supervisor immediately and record it in the deviation log within 24 hours. Critical deviations must be informed to QA within 1 hour.
## 3. Classification
Critical: a deviation that may affect patient safety or sterility assurance, for example a breach in aseptic processing. Major: a deviation that may affect product quality or a validated state, for example an out-of-specification result or equipment failure during a batch. Minor: a deviation with no impact on product quality, for example a late logbook entry.
## 4. Investigation
QA assigns an investigation owner. Root cause analysis uses 5-Why and Ishikawa (fishbone) tools. The impact on other batches must be assessed.
## 5. Timelines
Deviations must be closed within 30 calendar days. Extensions require QA Head approval with justification.
## 6. Batch Disposition
Batches affected by an open critical or major deviation remain on QA hold and cannot be released until the deviation is closed.
## 7. CAPA
Corrective and preventive actions are recorded in the CAPA system and their effectiveness is verified after implementation.
