# Gowning Procedure for Grade A/B Aseptic Areas

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To define the gowning sequence for personnel entering Grade B cleanrooms that surround Grade A aseptic filling zones.
## 2. Scope
Applies to all operators, engineers and QA staff entering the aseptic core of the injectable filling block.
## 3. Responsibility
Operators follow this procedure. The area supervisor verifies compliance. QA performs periodic gowning qualification.
## 4. Prerequisites
Personnel must hold a valid gowning qualification, renewed every 12 months, and must not enter if suffering from cold, skin lesions or diarrhoea. Jewellery, watches, cosmetics and mobile phones are not permitted beyond the primary change room.
## 5. Gowning Sequence
1. In the primary change room remove outer clothing and wear factory garments and dedicated footwear.
2. Wash hands with soap for at least 30 seconds and dry with a lint-free towel.
3. In the secondary change room sanitise hands with 70% IPA.
4. Put on the sterile hood, then the sterile coverall without touching the outer surface to the floor.
5. Put on sterile overboots while crossing the step-over bench.
6. Wear sterile goggles and a face mask so that no skin is exposed.
7. Wear the first pair of sterile gloves, then the second pair over the coverall cuffs.
8. Check the complete gown in the full-length mirror and sanitise gloves with sterile 70% IPA before entry.
## 6. Gloves Sanitisation
Gloves must be sanitised with sterile 70% IPA before every intervention in Grade A and at least every 15 minutes during continuous work.
## 7. Gown Change Frequency
Sterile garments are single use per entry. A gown must be changed immediately if it is torn, wet or contacts a non-sterile surface.
