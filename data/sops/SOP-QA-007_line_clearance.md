# Line Clearance for Filling and Packaging Lines

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To prevent mix-ups between batches and products by ensuring lines are cleared before starting a new batch.
## 2. When Line Clearance Is Required
Before the start of every batch, after product changeover and after maintenance on the line.
## 3. Procedure
1. Production removes all materials, labels, cartons and documents of the previous batch.
2. The line is cleaned as per the cleaning SOP and the status label is changed to CLEANED.
3. Production performs the line clearance check using the checklist and signs.
4. QA independently verifies the line and signs the batch record before the batch starts.
## 4. Labels and Printed Material
All leftover printed material of the previous batch must be reconciled and destroyed or returned to the warehouse under supervision.
