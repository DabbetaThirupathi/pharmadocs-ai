# Environmental Monitoring of Cleanrooms

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To define environmental monitoring (EM) of viable and non-viable particles in classified areas.
## 2. Methods
Non-viable particles are counted with a calibrated particle counter. Viable monitoring uses settle plates, active air sampling, contact plates and glove prints.
## 3. Action Limits for Grade A
Settle plates (90 mm, 4 hours): action limit 1 CFU. Active air: 1 CFU per cubic metre. Glove print (5 fingers): 1 CFU. Any growth in Grade A must be investigated.
## 4. Action Limits for Grade B
Settle plates: 5 CFU per 4 hours. Active air: 10 CFU per cubic metre. Glove print: 5 CFU.
## 5. Excursions
If an action limit is exceeded, QC informs QA and production the same day, the organism is identified to species level and a deviation is raised. The impact on batches filled during the excursion is assessed.
## 6. Trending
EM data are trended monthly and reviewed by QA.
