# Visual Inspection of Filled Vials

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To detect vials with visible particles, cracks, fill-volume defects or improper sealing.
## 2. Inspector Qualification
Inspectors must pass an eye test every year and a qualification with a defect kit every 6 months.
## 3. Inspection Conditions
Inspection is performed against black and white backgrounds with light intensity between 2000 and 3750 lux. Each vial is viewed for 5 seconds against each background.
## 4. Defect Categories
Critical defects: cracked vial, missing stopper, visible glass particle. Major defects: wrong fill volume, crimp defect. Minor defects: cosmetic marks on the cap.
## 5. Acceptance Quality Limit
After 100% inspection, a sample is checked by QA using AQL 0.10 for critical defects.
