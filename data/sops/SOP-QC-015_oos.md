# Investigation of Out-of-Specification Results

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To define the investigation of out-of-specification (OOS) analytical results.
## 2. Phase I Laboratory Investigation
The analyst informs the QC supervisor immediately. The supervisor checks calculations, instrument suitability, standards, and sample preparation. Retesting is not allowed before Phase I is complete.
## 3. Phase II Full Investigation
If no laboratory error is found, a full investigation including manufacturing review is initiated with QA. A deviation is raised.
## 4. Retesting
Retesting may only be done on the original sample by a second analyst when justified, with the number of retests predefined.
## 5. Reporting
OOS investigations must be completed within 30 days.
