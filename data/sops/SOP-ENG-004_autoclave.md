# Operation of Steam Sterilizer (Autoclave)

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To describe the operation of the double-door steam sterilizer used for sterilisation of filling machine parts, stoppers and garments.
## 2. Pre-operation Checks
Verify the sterilizer is within its calibration and requalification date. Check that the Bowie-Dick test performed at the start of the day has passed. Check that steam supply pressure is between 3.0 and 4.0 bar.
## 3. Loading
Load items only as per the validated loading pattern. Wrap parts in double steam-penetrable sterilisation wrap. Place a chemical indicator in each load.
## 4. Cycle Parameters
The validated cycle for porous loads is 121.1 °C for a minimum of 30 minutes exposure, followed by a vacuum drying phase of 20 minutes. Three pre-vacuum pulses are performed before exposure.
## 5. Unloading
Unload from the sterile side only. Verify the cycle printout shows PASS, check the chemical indicator colour change and record the cycle number in the sterilizer logbook.
## 6. Cycle Failure
If the cycle aborts or the printout shows FAIL, do not unload to the sterile side. Inform engineering and QA, raise a deviation and re-process the load after the cause is corrected.
## 7. Holding Time
Sterilised items must be used within 72 hours of sterilisation if the wrap remains intact. Items beyond 72 hours must be re-sterilised.
