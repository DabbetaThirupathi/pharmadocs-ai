# Receipt and Storage of Raw Materials

> Illustrative training document written for the PharmaDocs AI demo. Not an official SOP of any company.

## 1. Purpose
To describe the receipt, quarantine and storage of raw materials, active pharmaceutical ingredients and packaging materials.
## 2. Receipt
Check the vehicle condition, the delivery documents against the purchase order and the approved vendor list. Check container integrity and labels. For cold-chain materials verify the temperature data logger.
## 3. Quarantine
After goods receipt in the ERP system, materials are labelled QUARANTINE and stored in the quarantine area. The ERP automatically creates an inspection lot for QC sampling.
## 4. Release
Only after QC approval and the usage decision in the ERP system is the label changed to APPROVED and the stock moved to unrestricted use.
## 5. Storage Conditions
Store materials as per label conditions. Cold room: 2 to 8 °C. Controlled room temperature: not more than 25 °C. Temperature is monitored continuously.
## 6. Dispensing
Materials are dispensed on First Expiry First Out (FEFO) basis.
